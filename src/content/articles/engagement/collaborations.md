Collaboration is part of the iGEM spirit. This page records the meaningful work we did with other iGEM teams and outside partners, and what each side gained from it.

## What counts as a collaboration

A collaboration is a two-way exchange where both teams contribute and both benefit. For each one, note who we worked with, what we did together, and the concrete outcome: data, parts, protocols, feedback, or shared events.

## How to document a strong collaboration

- Show that the exchange was genuine and two-way, not a one-off message.
- Link to any shared data, parts, or protocols, and credit the originating team.
- If another team contributed to our project, also acknowledge them through the standardized form on the [Attributions](/attributions) page.
