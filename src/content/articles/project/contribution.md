This page documents the contribution BASIS-China makes to the wider iGEM
community: a concrete, reusable resource that future teams can pick up and apply
to their own projects with minimal friction.

> **Bronze Medal Criterion — Contribution.** Make a documented, reusable
> contribution that will help future iGEM teams (a part, characterization data, a
> protocol, software, or a model). See the
> [Medals page](https://competition.igem.org/judging/medals) for details.

## What our contribution is

Describe the contribution in one or two precise sentences so a stranger can
understand it without reading the rest of the wiki. Be concrete about its form:

- The exact artifact (e.g., a new BioBrick part, characterization dataset, wet-lab
  protocol, or analysis script).
- The biological or technical context it lives in.
- What was previously missing that this fills.

If the contribution builds on existing work, name the part, paper, or prior team
so credit is clear.

## Who it helps and why

State the audience and the problem you save them from solving again:

- Teams working on the same chassis, pathway, or measurement.
- Teams that need a validated starting point instead of an untested guess.
- Future BASIS-China members continuing this line of work.

Quantify the benefit where you can (time saved, conditions tested, replicates run).

## How a future team can reuse it

1. Locate the resource: link the part on the Registry and reference our
   [Results](/results) and [Engineering Success](/engineering) pages.
2. Read the assumptions: list the strain, growth conditions, and equipment used.
3. Reproduce or adapt: follow the protocol steps and substitute their own context.
4. Cite us: ask reusers to credit BASIS-China 2026 and link back to this page.

## Contribution summary

| Field         | Detail                                                |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Type          | Describe: part / data / protocol / software / model   |
| Identifier    | List the part number, dataset name, or repository     |
| Status        | Characterized, validated, or proof-of-concept         |
| Dependencies  | List strains, reagents, or tools required             |
| Reuse license | State the open license (e.g., the iGEM/OpenMTA terms) |

## Documentation quality checklist

- [ ] Plain-language summary of what the contribution does.
- [ ] Reproducible methods with units and conditions.
- [ ] Raw and processed data linked from [Results](/results).
- [ ] Known limitations and failure modes stated honestly.
- [ ] Clear reuse instructions and citation guidance.

## References

Cite the prior parts, papers, datasets, and teams your contribution builds on.
Use a consistent style and link to primary sources. List the Registry entry,
protocol DOI, or repository URL so reusers can find the canonical version.
