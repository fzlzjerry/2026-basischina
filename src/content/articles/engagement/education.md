This page documents how our team designs, delivers, and evaluates education and
outreach activities that invite the public into a genuine, two-way conversation
about synthetic biology.

> **Best Education Special Prize.** Strong education work informs audiences while
> learning from them, and reports both the activities and their measured impact.
> See the [Awards page](https://competition.igem.org/judging/awards) for details.

## Our education philosophy

We treat education as dialogue, not broadcast. Each activity is built to give
participants a way to ask questions, share concerns, and shape what we do next.
Connect this work to our broader [Human Practices](/human-practices) so readers
see how listening fed back into the project.

- State the central learning goal for each audience.
- Describe how the activity creates a feedback loop, not a one-way lecture.
- Note what you changed in the project or materials because of what you heard.
