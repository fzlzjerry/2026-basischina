This page records exactly what each member of BASIS-China contributed and acknowledges everyone outside the team who helped us. The team designed, executed, and documented the core of the project ourselves; outside support was advisory, technical, or logistical, and is credited transparently below.

> **Bronze Medal Criterion — Attributions.** Clearly state the work your team
> did and acknowledge all outside help you received. See the
> [Medals page](https://competition.igem.org/judging/medals) for details.

## How to read this page

- Every major work area maps to one or more named team members.
- "Outside help" means anything not done by a registered student team member, including advice, lab access, training, and gifted materials.
- Honest, specific attribution is required for the medal and is also a courtesy to the people who supported us.

## What the team did itself

The students of BASIS-China carried out the core work of this project, including:

- Project ideation, literature review, and experimental design.
- All wet-lab cloning, assays, and data collection. See [Experiments](/experiments).
- All modeling, software, and data analysis. See the [Model](/model) page.
- [Human Practices](/human-practices), education, and outreach activities.
- Wiki content, the [Results](/results) write-up, and final documentation.

## Acknowledgements

We thank the people and organizations who supported us. For each, note the name, affiliation, and the specific help given.

1. **Principal Investigator(s).** Name the PI and the guidance or lab oversight provided.
2. **Instructors.** List teachers who mentored the team and what they advised on.
3. **Advisors.** List technical advisors and the specific expertise they shared.
4. **External collaborators.** List other iGEM teams, labs, or experts, and describe any exchanged data, parts, or protocols.
5. **Sponsors and facilities.** List groups that donated reagents, equipment, funding, or lab space.

If another iGEM team contributed, also describe that exchange on your [Collaborations](/collaborations) page.
