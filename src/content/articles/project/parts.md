iGEM judges all part work from the iGEM Registry, not from the wiki: the official record for every part lives on its Registry part page, and you enter the part numbers on the Judging Form. This page is an optional summary that links to our Registry parts so visitors can find them in one place.

> **Where parts are judged.** All part documentation you want evaluated for the
> part prizes must live on the part's page in the iGEM Registry. See the
